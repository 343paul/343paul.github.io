Privacy Policy

Stock Hero · effective October 20, 2026

Who uses Stock Hero

Stock Hero is a personal tool used only by the owner of the GreenOrDieTrying YouTube channel, on the owner's own computer. It does not sign in, collect information from, or serve any other person.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, given through Google's sign-in, Stock Hero uses the YouTube Data API to manage the owner's own GreenOrDieTrying channel: it sets the title and thumbnail of the channel's live streams, creates each day's live broadcast, uploads short videos the owner has made, and reads the channel's own broadcast status and viewer count.

What it keeps

The sign-in permission is stored only on the owner's computer. Stock Hero does not collect, store, sell or share personal information, and it does not pass YouTube data to anyone else. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time in the Google Account's security settings, under third-party connections, by removing Stock Hero.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the GreenOrDieTrying YouTube channel.
