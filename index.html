Stock Hero

The live small-cap stock scanner behind the GreenOrDieTrying YouTube channel.

Stock Hero is a personal tool run by the owner of the GreenOrDieTrying YouTube channel. It shows a live scanner of the day's top small-cap gainers, and with the owner's permission it manages that one channel: setting the daily live stream's title and thumbnail, preparing each morning's broadcast, and uploading short videos of the day's top gainers.

It is not offered to the public and does not sign in anyone else.

Watch the scanner live every trading day, 4 AM – 8 PM ET: youtube.com/@greenordietrying/live
